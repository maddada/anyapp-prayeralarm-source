# Prayer Alarm: overview

A Muslim prayer alarm for the place the person chooses. Android only.

## What it is for
- Show today's prayer times for the chosen place, right every day.
- Speak reminders before each prayer and play the adhan at the prayer time, even when the app
  is closed, and keep doing so every day without the app ever being opened.

## Place
- No city is built in. The place is a setting (Settings, Times tab): use the phone's location,
  pick a capital city from a list, or type a latitude and longitude.
- If no place is saved yet, the app asks once when it opens. Prayer times, the Qibla, the
  notification and every screen use the chosen place.

## Main screens (bottom bar with three icons only: prayer times, Qibla, Settings)
- Prayer times: date and Islamic (Hijri) date, the next prayer with the time left in very large
  type ("2 h 14 min") under its name, then the five prayers plus Shuruq at the same size, each
  with its own icon and (except Shuruq) a sound icon to switch its alarms on or off.
- Qibla: a compass that turns with the phone, a Kaaba mark pointing to Makkah, "Turn left/right"
  or "Facing the Qibla". Uses the phone's position, or the chosen place.
  Shows the compass accuracy; when it is low, an "Improve accuracy" button opens an animated
  figure-8 guide to calibrate the compass, with the accuracy updating live.
- Settings: four tabs at the top (Alarms, Times, Display, About; credits and "This app" only in
  About).
  - Alarms: alarms on/off, two spoken reminders with minutes chosen by the person (default 20
    and 5), adhan voice (four recordings) and adhan length, with Play adhan and Hear reminder
    side by side.
  - Times: place, calculation method and Asr school, "Match my mosque", per-prayer adjustment
    in minutes.
  - Display: status bar notification, Islamic date correction (-2 to +2 days), 24-hour clock.
- Mosque board: take or choose a photo of the mosque's prayer board; the phone reads the times
  (on the phone, no account), the person checks them, and they become the per-prayer adjustments.

## Times
- Several calculation methods (UAE Awqaf is the default; Karachi, Muslim World League, Umm
  al-Qura, Egypt and others), Standard or Hanafi Asr.
- The person's own minute adjustments for each prayer are added on top.

## Alarm sound
- Each prayer has a sound icon on the home screen to tap on or off: off means no reminders and
  no adhan for that prayer; its time still shows.
- Flipping the phone face down stops any adhan or spoken reminder that is playing.
- Adhan length: "Short" (only the first "Allahu akbar, Allahu akbar") is the default, or the
  full adhan; works for every adhan voice.

## Notification
- A quiet, always-there notification: five times in a row with the next one highlighted
  (Shuruq replaces Isha from after Isha until sunrise has passed; otherwise the usual five),
  one fixed text size so every time shows in full, the Islamic date in the header, and the next
  prayer's time in the status bar (hour on top, minutes under it; 12-hour or 24-hour).

## Choices that matter
- Reminders are spoken in English ("Fajr prayer is in 20 minutes.").
- Calm design: deep green accent on warm paper, dark mode, mosque icon.
- No em dashes anywhere.
